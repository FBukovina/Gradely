Gradey 2.1 makes planning your school week easier.

• New Planner for homework, tests, presentations, notes and tasks.
• Create plans directly from your timetable and set a deadline for the next lesson.
• Add dated plans to Apple Calendar and keep their events up to date from Gradey.
• Switch between the weekly and permanent Bakaláři timetable.
• Improved privacy: this version links Bakaláři accounts without uploading your password to Gradey's servers.
• Redeem support subscription offer codes directly in the app.
• Start using Gradey without creating a Gradey ID, and find clearer legal links on macOS.
