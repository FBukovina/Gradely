Gradey 2.2 brings a clearer daily overview, more flexible grade planning, and new Siri and Shortcuts actions.

## What's new

- **Improved Today:** see current and upcoming lessons, timetable changes, recent marks, and Planner items together.
- **Grade simulator:** try several hypothetical grades at once or explore what you need to reach a target average.
- **Siri and Shortcuts:** check lessons, grades, and Planner items, open relevant screens, or add a Planner item with confirmation.
- **Optional Spotlight discovery:** choose whether school content appears in Siri and Spotlight from Settings → Privacy & Data.
- **Daily Planner reminders:** enable an evening summary of tomorrow's planned items.
- **More control over AI context:** choose the school information attached to a conversation, with a separate choice for test notes. General chats do not automatically attach school records.

## Improvements

- Refined English, Czech, and Chronically Online translations.
- Better layouts and readable controls at larger text sizes.
- More reliable refreshing, cached data, and account switching, including separate school context for EduPage children.

## Build

The iPhone/iPad build **2.2 (4)**, including the Watch app, was uploaded to App Store Connect on September 22, 2026. The Release archive, runtime configuration, embedded bundle versions and signatures, Siri metadata, and localization checks passed.
