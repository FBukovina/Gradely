Gradey 2.5 adds optional TŠ Vavruška dance classes and a more native Mac experience.

## What's new

- **TŠ Vavruška dance classes:** connect your youth course in Settings to see upcoming and past lessons, ticket sale times, and notice board updates.
- **Dance classes in your day:** Today shows your next lesson, and weekday lessons appear after school in the Timetable, including the Mac week grid.
- **Ticket reminders:** enable a local notification when ticket sales open, then follow the link to the ticket page.
- **Course connection:** your calendar link is kept in the device Keychain; your course password is not stored. If the link stops working, Gradey keeps cached lessons visible and asks you to reconnect.

## Better on Mac

- A dedicated Settings window, opened with Command-comma.
- Menu commands and keyboard shortcuts for switching sections, refreshing, creating planner items, and opening Gradey AI.
- A full-week Timetable grid and a side-by-side subject list and marks view.
- Controls and content widths adapted to Mac, with a single main window and standard Dock behavior.
