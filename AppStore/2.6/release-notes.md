Gradey 2.6 adds friend timetables: see a friend's or partner's week next to yours.

## What's new

- **Friends in Timetable:** tap the Timetable title to switch between your timetable and a friend's. Their week uses the same view as yours, including substitutions and room changes, and lessons open read-only.
- **Three ways to add a friend:**
  - **Bakaláři login:** your friend signs in once on your device. Gradey only loads their timetable, never marks or absences, and the login stays in this device's Keychain.
  - **School's public timetable:** pick their school and class, no password needed, then choose which language and PE groups they're in. Works for schools that publish their timetable.
  - **PDF or photo:** Gradey reads a timetable printed from Bakaláři (or a sharp photo of one) on the device, then lets you check it, fix any lesson, switch off groups and adjust lesson times before saving. You can also type a timetable in by hand.
- **Settings → Connected services** lists your friends, where each timetable comes from, and lets you rename or remove them. Signing out removes friends' logins from the device.
- **Weekly and permanent timetable** now sit in the Timetable title menu on iPhone, next to whose timetable you're looking at.
